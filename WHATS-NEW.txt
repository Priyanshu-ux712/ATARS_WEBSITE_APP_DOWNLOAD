Every saved analysis can now produce a Certificate of Verified Analysis: one page
stating what was in the file, what was done to it, and whether the numbers still
match the data. Each sheet carries a signed QR code, and ATARS can read those codes
back — from the PDF or from a photo of a printed page — and re-check them against the
data itself. A certificate that has been edited stops verifying.

Also fixed in this update:
• True/False columns no longer crash the report
• .pkl files open again, through a loader that refuses unsafe pickles
• Nested JSON loads as the table inside it, instead of one unusable column
• "Save as" keeps every sheet, including the empty ones
